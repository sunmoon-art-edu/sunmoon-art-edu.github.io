介绍 Introduction

More than a Chinese class.

Different ages.Different needs.One meaningful journey.

At SunMoon, we believe children learn a language best when they are genuinely curious about it.

That is why our classrooms are designed around conversation, stories, creativity, movement and real interaction, not simply memorising vocabulary.

From a child's first Chinese word to a confident conversation, every stage has its own way of learning.

家长 For Parents

What does my child actually need?

The questions we hear most often from parents, answered honestly.

My child is only 3. Is it too early?

No.

At this stage, the goal is not academic performance. It is creating familiarity, listening ability and positive associations with Mandarin.

My child has never learned Chinese before.

That's okay.

We can start from zero and build the foundation step by step.

How do I know which class is right?

We don't ask parents to guess.

After understanding the child's age, experience and current ability, SunMoon will recommend the appropriate learning path.

问答 FAQ

Questions parents ask

Still unsure? Ask us directly. We answer every message personally.

What age can my child start?

From 3 years old.

Does my child need previous Chinese experience?

No. We have programmes for complete beginners.

How do you assess a child's level?

Through a short consultation and/or a trial session.

Do you offer private classes?

Yes.

Do you offer HSK preparation?

Yes. Our HSK Pathway runs from Foundation to HSK 6.

Can adults learn Chinese?

Yes. Adult programmes are personalised according to each learner's goals.

Do you offer Business Chinese?

Yes.

How many students are in one class?

At most 6 children per class, so every child gets the teacher’s attention.
